Landlord Accounting Services Glasgow: How We Can Help

By Sardar Muhammad, AAT • 11 September 2026 • 8 min read

Running a rental property involves more than collecting rent

Landlords must keep reliable records, report rental income correctly and meet HMRC deadlines. The right approach depends on whether you own one property, a portfolio, a jointly owned property or a property company. We help landlords in Glasgow and across Scotland organise the numbers and make informed decisions.

How we support landlords

  • Prepare rental income and expense schedules for Self Assessment.
  • Review repairs, insurance, letting fees, safety certificates and professional costs.
  • Explain the difference between revenue repairs and capital improvements.
  • Check mortgage interest tax relief and how Section 24 affects your position.
  • Keep records ready if HMRC asks questions about your property income.
  • Help compare personal ownership with a limited company before you restructure.

Tax records that make returns easier

Keep a separate record for each property. Record the rent received, date and description of every cost, the property it relates to, and whether it is a repair, finance cost or capital item. Bank statements alone are not enough if they do not explain the transaction.

For jointly owned property, keep evidence of the ownership split and how income and expenses are allocated. If a property is used personally for part of the year, private use must also be separated from the business figures.

Planning before you buy or transfer property

Tax advice is most useful before a purchase, incorporation or transfer. We can help you compare the likely tax and administration of personal ownership and a company, while highlighting issues such as mortgage finance, capital gains tax, stamp duty and extraction of profits. The best structure depends on your circumstances, not just the headline tax rate.

When should a landlord speak to an accountant?

Speak to us before letting a new property, buying through a company, remortgaging, transferring ownership, receiving an HMRC letter or missing a deadline. Early advice usually gives you more options and better records.

Landlord tax support in Glasgow

LimeTree Accounting Solutions can prepare your property tax figures, explain your obligations and deal with the practical accounting work behind your return.

Frequently asked questions

Do landlords need an accountant?

No. You can prepare your own return, but an accountant can help identify allowable costs, separate repairs from improvements and reduce the risk of missed deadlines or incomplete records.

Can you help with more than one rental property?

Yes. We can keep separate schedules for each property and combine the figures correctly for your tax return.

Can you advise on forming a property company?

We can explain the accounting and tax questions to consider and work with you on the figures. Legal and mortgage advice may also be needed before a transfer.
