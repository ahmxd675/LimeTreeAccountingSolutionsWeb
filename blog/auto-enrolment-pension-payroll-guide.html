Auto-Enrolment Pension Guide for Small Employers

By Sardar Muhammad, AAT • 11 September 2026 • 7 min read

Hiring staff creates pension duties

Employers must assess eligible workers for automatic enrolment and meet workplace pension responsibilities. The duties can apply even when you employ only one person. Payroll data, worker age, earnings and joining dates all matter.

What employers need to organise

  • Choose a qualifying workplace pension scheme.
  • Assess workers when they start and at each relevant pay period.
  • Automatically enrol eligible workers and make the required contributions.
  • Send the correct employee and contribution information to the pension provider.
  • Tell staff about their rights and keep records of communications.
  • Complete re-enrolment and declaration tasks when required.

Why payroll and pensions need to agree

Payroll is usually where pension deductions and employer contributions are calculated. Errors can arise when a worker's status changes, pay includes overtime or statutory payments, or an employee opts in or out. Reconcile payroll reports to provider schedules so discrepancies are found early.

Common small-business mistakes

  • Assuming a low-paid or part-time worker can never be eligible.
  • Forgetting to assess a new starter.
  • Using the wrong pensionable pay basis in payroll.
  • Missing an opt-in request or postponement date.
  • Failing to keep evidence of letters, assessments and payments.

How an accountant can help

We can help coordinate payroll information, check pension deductions against payroll reports and keep an employer timetable. Pension scheme advice and legal questions should be confirmed with the provider or a specialist adviser, but good accounting records make compliance much easier.

A clear routine keeps pension administration manageable

Set one monthly process for payroll changes, pension reports, payments and employee communications.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a workplace pension for one employee?

You may have duties even with one employee. Eligibility depends on factors including age, earnings and worker status.

Can payroll software handle auto-enrolment?

Many payroll systems can calculate deductions and produce information, but you still need to configure the scheme correctly and complete employer duties.

Can you set up the pension scheme?

We can help organise payroll information and explain the accounting side. The employer remains responsible for choosing a scheme and meeting the legal duties.
