Payroll Services for Small Businesses in Glasgow

By Sardar Muhammad, AAT • 11 September 2026 • 7 min read

Payroll should be accurate, private and on time

Paying staff creates regular responsibilities for an employer. You need to calculate gross pay and deductions, send the right information to HMRC, issue payslips and keep payroll records. A mistake can affect an employee's pay, tax code or confidence in your business.

What our payroll service can cover

  • Set up PAYE and payroll records for a new employer.
  • Calculate salaries, hourly pay, overtime, statutory payments and deductions.
  • Prepare payslips and submit Real Time Information (RTI) to HMRC.
  • Process starters, leavers, tax codes and workplace changes.
  • Provide payroll reports for bookkeeping and year-end accounts.
  • Coordinate payroll information with workplace pension duties.

Why RTI matters

Employers generally report payments and deductions to HMRC on or before the payday. The submission must match the actual payroll, including employee details, pay, tax and National Insurance. Late or incorrect information can cause avoidable corrections, penalties or tax-code problems.

Payroll for directors and small teams

Payroll is not only for larger employers. A company director taking a salary may need to be included, and a small team still requires consistent records. We can set up a payroll timetable, confirm what information is needed each month and keep the process manageable.

Payroll and workplace pensions

Employers may also have automatic-enrolment duties. Payroll information helps determine qualifying earnings, contributions and employee status, but pension duties are separate from PAYE. We can help organise the payroll figures and explain what information you need to keep.

When outsourcing payroll makes sense

Outsourcing can be useful when you are hiring your first employee, changing payroll software, paying variable hours, dealing with statutory leave or simply spending too much time checking calculations. You keep control of the business while we handle the recurring administration.

Payroll support for Glasgow employers

We can agree a payroll routine that fits your business, keep records organised and give you a clear contact for payroll questions.

Frequently asked questions

Can you run payroll for one employee?

Yes. A small payroll still needs accurate PAYE records, RTI submissions and payslips, so it can be worthwhile to outsource from the start.

How often do you need payroll information?

That depends on your pay frequency. We can agree a monthly or weekly deadline for hours, changes, expenses and other information.

Can you help set up a new PAYE scheme?

Yes. We can help organise the employer details and payroll process, then explain the information you need to provide each pay period.
