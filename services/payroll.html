Payroll and Workplace Pensions

Good payroll records make employer compliance easier to manage.

Payroll information is often used to calculate pension deductions and employer contributions. We can help reconcile payroll figures and pension reports, organise the information your provider needs and keep a regular process for payroll changes.

Workplace pension duties remain the employer's responsibility, including choosing a qualifying scheme, assessing workers and communicating with employees. We can support the accounting and payroll side while you retain control of the employer decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you run payroll for one employee?

Yes. Even a one-person payroll needs accurate PAYE records, payslips and RTI submissions.

How often do you need payroll information?

That depends on whether you pay weekly or monthly. We agree a deadline for hours, changes, expenses and other information before each run.

Can you help with director payroll?

Yes. We can include directors in the payroll process and coordinate salary records with the company's accounts and tax planning.
