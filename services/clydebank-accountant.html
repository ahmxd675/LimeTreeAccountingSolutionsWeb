Self-Assessment tax returns in Clydebank

Self-Assessment is the service Clydebank clients ask us about most. We prepare and file tax returns for:

  • Sole traders and tradespeople — builders, joiners, electricians, plumbers, mechanics and cleaners
  • Taxi and delivery drivers — private hire, Uber, Bolt, Just Eat and Deliveroo
  • Landlords with one or more rental properties
  • CIS subcontractors claiming back overpaid tax
  • Company directors and anyone with income outside PAYE

Online returns for the 2025/26 tax year are due by 31 January 2027. From April 2026, sole traders and landlords with income over £50,000 also need to follow Making Tax Digital — we can set this up for you. More about our Self-Assessment service.
